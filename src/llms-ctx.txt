# Whizz Systems Developer & Content Context

This file outlines the internal rules, engineering standards, and structural guidelines for generating and maintaining the Whizz Systems web platform.

## Architecture & Rendering
- **Tech Stack:** Angular (Standalone Components), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vercel.
- **Rendering:** SSR (Server-Side Rendering) for SEO optimization, Pre-rendering for static-like performance on marketing and informational pages.
- **Paths:** `src/app/pages/` for layouts, `src/app/shared/components/` for UI, `src/assets/` for local media/PDFs.

## Content, AEO, & GEO Guidelines
- **Intent Coverage:** Content must explicitly fulfill transactional, commercial, and informational intents (specifically targeting engineers and technical users via white papers).
- **AI Formatting:** Use clear question-based H2/H3 headings followed by direct 40–60 word answer blocks. Include technical statistics and process explanations.
- **Schema Requirements:** Deploy `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQ`, and `TechArticle` or `DigitalDocument` markup for engineering assets.

## Core Web Vitals Targets
- LCP < 2.5s
- CLS < 0.1
- INP < 200ms

## System Constraints (DO NOT)
- Do not create duplicate pages for the same keyword.
- Do not break the component design system.
- Do not use inline CSS.
- Do not block white paper landing pages or major service structures in `robots.txt`.
